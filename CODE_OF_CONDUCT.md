# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want this project to be a welcoming, harassment-free place for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or
invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education,
socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and
orientation.

This project helps people control a computer without a mouse, so people with disabilities may be among its most
important users and contributors. Please keep that in mind.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that helps:

- Showing empathy and kindness toward other people
- Respecting different opinions, viewpoints and experiences
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Accepting responsibility for mistakes, apologizing, and learning from them
- Focusing on what is best for the whole community

Examples of unacceptable behaviour:

- Sexualized language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information, such as a physical or email address, without their explicit permission
- Other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Scope and enforcement

This code applies in all project spaces, and when someone is officially representing the project elsewhere. Project
maintainers may remove, edit or reject contributions that don't follow it, and may temporarily or permanently ban
anyone whose behaviour is harmful.

## Reporting

To report a problem, contact the maintainer privately using the details on their GitHub profile. If there are none, open
an issue asking for a private way to get in touch, without describing the problem. Reports are handled as promptly and
confidentially as possible, and anyone reporting in good faith will be protected from retaliation.
